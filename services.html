From walk-in to week one

How a program actually gets built.

The same four steps for every discipline on the board — nobody skips the assessment.

Step 01

Movement assessment

45 minutes on the floor with a coach — mobility, history, the shoulder that keeps barking.

Step 02

Program written

A named coach writes your first 4-week block against your numbers, not a template.

Step 03

Coached sessions

Train on the capped floor. Your coach is present, not just supervising from a desk.

Step 04

Monthly review

Numbers checked, block adjusted. Programs change when your life or lifts do.
