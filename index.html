The Stackly standard

Four refusals we still stand by.

01

No ghost coaching

If a coach's name is on your plan, they wrote it — and they're on the floor when you train it.

02

No pressure upsells

We won't chase you to a supplement wall. Ask us and we'll tell you plainly, not sell you loudly.

03

No overbooked floor

Class sizes are capped and printed on the board — not adjusted quietly when it's convenient.

04

No hidden lock-in

Month-to-month as standard. Annual only if it genuinely saves you money, explained in writing.
